Facts

Eight answers about the verifier
QuestionAnswer
What it doesChecks financial claims against SEC filings; returns verdicts and cited corrections.用 SEC 申報核對財務主張，提供判定與附出處的修正。
Time spanSEC XBRL periods, generally 2009 onward, through the latest filing.SEC XBRL 申報期間，大致自 2009 年起至最新申報。
Which US stocksUS-GAAP SEC filers: ≈8,000 companies; 23 tested.US-GAAP 美股申報公司：約 8,000 家，已測試 23 家。
InputsWebsite: text ≤600 characters. CLI: text, images, PDFs, audio and video.網站：≤600 字元。CLI：文字、圖片、PDF、音訊與影片。
Price$0.01 per live check; $0.02 per claim in the benchmark.即時查核每次 $0.01；基準測試每題 $0.02。
Speed6 s live; 10 s median in the benchmark.即時 6 秒；基準測試中位數 10 秒。
Accuracy90.2% (240/266) held out; false support 1/139.保留測試 90.2% (240/266)；誤判為支持 1/139。
Fix the article?Corrects disproved numbers and cites the filing.修正被證明錯誤的數字，並附申報出處。
