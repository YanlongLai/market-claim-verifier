Facts

Eight answers about the verifier
QuestionAnswer
What it doesExtracts each financial claim → Muse Spark picks SEC tools → Python computes the verdict (supported / contradicted / insufficient evidence) → corrected draft for contradicted numbers.
Time spanAny period the company has reported in SEC XBRL (generally 2009 onward, earlier when restated) up to its latest 10-Q/10-K. Annual, quarterly and growth comparisons. Live SEC data refreshes every 6 h.
Which US stocksUS-listed companies filing 10-K/10-Q with US-GAAP XBRL — the SEC ticker map lists ≈8,000 companies. Deeply tested on 23. Not ETFs/funds; IFRS foreign filers limited.
InputsWebsite: pasted text ≤600 characters. CLI: text, screenshots (Muse OCR), PDFs with text, audio/video (Muse Voice Transcribe).
Price≈$0.01 per live check (measured $0.0099); $0.020 per claim on the 266-claim benchmark (Baseten).
SpeedLive: 6.3 s (verified production run). Benchmark: median 10 s, p95 21 s per claim.
Accuracy90.2% (240/266) on held-out claims; wrongly says "supported" 1 in 139 times. Benchmark text, not a guarantee on every post; real-world coverage reported separately.
Fix the article?Yes, for numbers it disproves: a corrected draft strikes the wrong number, inserts the filed value and cites the filing. Only checked assertions are changed; never labelled "approved".

Real-world coverage

Coverage ≠ accuracy. Real posts include unchecked assertions and abstentions. Primary truth labels and scope-only expectations are reported separately; post-hoc rechecks are not held out and do not retest OCR/ASR.
