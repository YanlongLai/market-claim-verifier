Decisions

  • One filled button in the section: Run check. Everything else is a segmented control, outline card or text button.
  • Selection is shown by a raised segment or accent outline, never a solid green block.
  • The right column answers two questions: what is running now (Now card) and what ran before (Recent checks).
  • Stepper is the at-a-glance answer; the server log is the proof underneath it.
  • History rows are two lines: what was checked, then provider · time · cost. Device and exact UTC move into the row's modal.
  • Fine print shrinks to one line plus a Details disclosure.
