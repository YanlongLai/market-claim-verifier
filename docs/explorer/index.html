Verification workflow

Model proposes; program decides

Single-shot answers without tools. The agent retrieves SEC facts and cites evidence IDs; program rules independently parse the claim's numbers, precision and comparisons to determine the final verdict.

  1. ProgramSplit assertionsCheck each numeric assertion separately.
  2. ModelRetrieve SEC factsCall lookup_fact, compute_change, Receive numbered evidence E1, E2…
  3. ProgramParse original claim“$143.1B”→ Value and precision ±$0.05B; “More than” implies an inequality; the program checks wording independently.
  4. ProgramValidate evidenceRequire valid evidence IDs and check fiscal/calendar interpretations.
  5. ProgramDetermine verdictReturn supported, contradicted or insufficient evidence, with filing strength and derivation labels.

Methods and limitations

Preregistered labels

Labels were committed before v2 runs (5d90b3b). Two Amazon labels were later corrected after trailing-twelve-month values were mistaken for annual values. Original and corrected reports remain available; scores differ by at most two claims.

Why single-shot scores are low

Without retrieval, models often returned insufficient evidence for 2025–2026 financial facts. Meta single-shot falsely supported 9 of 186 non-supported claims.

Original news sources

News examples retain a short paraphrase and source link, not full articles. Evidence displays SEC XBRL facts with concepts, periods, forms and accession identifiers.

v2 limitations

Record/first claims were difficult; Meta sometimes checked only one interpretation of an ambiguous quarter. Post-hoc results are shown separately. Main charts and the six-system comparison retain original v2 results.
